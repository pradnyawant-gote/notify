## Product principles

1. The day is the organizing unit. Habits, tasks, meetings, meals and rest share the same timeline.
2. Capture is brief. Title, time and duration are enough; recurrence and reminder controls stay optional.
3. Intelligence is explainable. A gap finder suggests free time; a deterministic notification planner honors budget, spacing, sleep and focus.
4. Calm is functional. Gentle mode defaults to four reminders/day, balanced to eight, proactive to twelve. Completion cancels pending reminders.
5. Privacy begins locally. Persist plans on the device without accounts or remote analytics.
6. Review without judgment. Show completed, still open and missed work separately; invite a small adjustment for tomorrow.
